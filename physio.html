Physiotherapy FAQ examples

Common questions Zennora can help answer.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

The AI receptionist can share the clinic's approved referral information while reminding visitors that insurer or program requirements may differ.

Do you direct bill extended health benefits?

It can explain the clinic's general direct-billing process and direct coverage-specific questions to staff.

Do you treat ICBC or WorkSafe patients?

Where the clinic offers those programs, it can share approved intake information and guide the patient to the appropriate next step.

Which service should I book?

It can explain the services the clinic offers, but it should not diagnose the patient's condition or choose an individualized treatment plan.

What should I bring to my first visit?

It can share the clinic's approved checklist, including forms or referral and claim information when applicable.

Can I book online?

It can guide patients to the clinic's existing booking page or preferred contact method.
