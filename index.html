Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will the AI answer only from our information?

It can be configured around your approved website content, FAQs and operating rules, with handoff messages for questions outside its scope.

Can it capture leads?

Yes. It can collect selected details such as name, contact information, service interest and preferred follow-up method.

Do we need to rebuild our website?

No. In most cases, a small script can add the assistant to your existing website.

Can the responses be short and natural?

Yes. Tone, response length and conversation rules can be customized to fit your brand.

Does it replace staff?

It works best as a front-line assistant for routine questions and lead capture, while people handle complex or sensitive conversations.
