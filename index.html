Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will the AI answer only from our information?

It can be configured around your approved website content, FAQs and operating rules, with staff-follow-up messages for questions outside its scope.

Can it capture leads?

Lead and inquiry capture is available on Growth and custom plans. Starter focuses on answering questions and guiding visitors toward your existing contact or booking options.

Do we need to rebuild our website?

No. In most cases, a small script can add the AI receptionist to your existing website.

Can the responses be short and natural?

Yes. Tone, response length and conversation rules can be customized to fit your brand.

Does it replace staff?

It works best as a front-line assistant for routine questions and next-step guidance while people handle complex, sensitive or high-value conversations.

What happens during the personalized preview?

We prepare an unlisted demonstration page using your public information and agreed scenarios. It is for evaluation and does not require a change to your live website.
