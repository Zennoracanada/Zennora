Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will the AI answer only from our information?

It can be configured around your approved website content, FAQs and operating rules. Clear limits and handoff messages help prevent unsupported answers.

Can it capture leads?

Yes. It can collect selected details such as name, contact information, service interest and preferred follow-up method.

Do we need to rebuild our website?

No. In most cases, a small website script can add the assistant to an existing site.

Can the responses sound concise?

Yes. Tone, response length and conversation rules can be customized to fit your brand.

Is it a replacement for staff?

It is best used as a front-line assistant for routine questions and lead capture, with people handling complex, sensitive or high-value conversations.
