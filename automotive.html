Automotive response boundaries

Service information—not a remote mechanical diagnosis.

The AI receptionist should use business-approved information, avoid diagnosing vehicle problems from symptoms alone and avoid promising final pricing where an inspection, parts availability or technician approval is required.

  • No guessed mechanical diagnosis
  • No guaranteed repair price unless explicitly published
  • Clear inspection and staff handoff
  • Minimal information collection

Automotive FAQ examples

Common questions Zennora can help answer.

Do you offer oil changes or regular maintenance?

The AI receptionist can confirm services listed by the business and guide the customer to the appropriate booking option.

Can I book a tire change online?

It can guide customers to the shop's existing tire-service booking or contact process.

Can I request a repair estimate?

It can explain the shop's quote process or collect selected vehicle and contact details where configured, without promising a final price before inspection.

My check-engine light is on. What is wrong?

It should not guess the cause. It can explain the shop's diagnostic service and guide the customer toward an inspection.

Can I drop my vehicle off after hours?

It can share the business's approved hours and after-hours drop-off instructions when those are published.

What information can it collect?

Growth and custom plans can collect selected details such as contact information, vehicle make/model and requested service, depending on the approved workflow.
