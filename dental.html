Canadian dental FAQ examples

Common questions Zennora can help answer.

Are you accepting new patients?

The assistant can explain whether your clinic is welcoming new patients and direct them to your preferred booking process.

Do you accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan?

It can share your clinic-approved CDCP information and avoid making promises about a patient’s individual eligibility or coverage.

Do you offer direct billing to insurance?

It can explain your clinic’s billing process and invite the patient to contact the clinic for plan-specific details.

I have severe tooth pain. Can I come today?

It can acknowledge the concern, share your emergency instructions and guide the patient to call, book or request urgent follow-up.

Do you provide Invisalign or teeth whitening?

It can explain whether the service is offered and guide the patient toward a consultation.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

It can provide your clinic’s checklist, such as photo identification, insurance information and previous records when applicable.
