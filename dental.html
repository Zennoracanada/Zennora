Canadian dental FAQ examples

Common questions Zennora can help answer.

Are you accepting new patients?

The AI receptionist can share your clinic-approved new-patient information and guide the patient to your preferred booking process.

Do you accept the Canadian Dental Care Plan?

It can share approved CDCP information while avoiding promises about individual eligibility or coverage.

Do you offer direct billing to insurance?

It can explain the clinic’s general billing process and direct plan-specific questions to staff.

I have severe tooth pain. Can I come today?

It can share the clinic’s approved urgent instructions and direct the patient to call. It should not diagnose or recommend treatment.

Do you provide Invisalign or teeth whitening?

It can explain whether the service is offered and guide the patient toward a consultation.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

It can share the clinic’s checklist, such as identification, insurance information and previous records when applicable.

Can it collect patient details?

Growth and custom plans can collect selected inquiry details. The workflow should minimize information and avoid collecting confidential health details in a general website conversation.
