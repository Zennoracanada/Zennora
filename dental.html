Dental FAQ

What clinic owners usually ask

Can it give dental advice?

It should not diagnose or recommend treatment. It can share approved general information and direct clinical questions to the clinic team.

Can it book appointments directly?

That depends on the clinic’s scheduling system and available integrations. A simpler starting point is collecting a structured appointment request for staff follow-up.

Can we control every answer?

You can define source information, tone, prohibited topics, escalation messages and response length. Testing remains important before launch.

Will it work with our current website?

Usually yes. The chat widget can commonly be installed using a small script added by the website administrator.

What happens when it does not know an answer?

It can say that it does not have enough information and offer a safe handoff rather than guessing.
